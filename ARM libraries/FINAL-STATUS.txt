BUILD METHOD SUCCESS:
---------------------

Our "NO-GYP" approach worked perfectly:

1. For simple libraries:
   - Just use xcodebuild with ARM64 flags
   - No modifications needed
   
2. For libraries with x86 code:
   - Copy Xcode project to ARM build folder
   - Remove x86-specific files
   - Build with xcodebuild

3. For libraries with ARM assembly:
   - Just use xcodebuild with ARM64 flags
   - Native ARM support already present!
